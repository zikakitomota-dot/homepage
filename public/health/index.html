Choose the estimate that matches your question

A formula predicts from the values entered; it does not measure your body or diagnose health. Start with the question you are trying to answer, then read the method and limitations on that calculator before interpreting the number.

  • Current weight relative to height: BMI is an adult screening measure, not a body-composition test.
  • Resting versus full-day energy: BMR predicts resting energy; TDEE adds an activity multiplier and therefore adds more uncertainty.
  • A mathematical weight-loss scenario: Calorie Deficit subtracts a selected deficit from estimated TDEE. It is not a diet prescription.
  • Historical height-based formulas: Reference Weight compares three traditional equations; it does not identify a perfect or medically required weight.
  • Activity or hydration context: Walking Calories uses MET values, while Water Intake provides a rough total-water planning estimate. Both vary substantially between individuals.

How methods and sources are handled

Zalea Studio publishes and maintains this Health Calculators collection. Calculator pages identify the equation or reference method, link to primary research, government guidance, or established reference works where available, and state when a familiar multiplier or conversion is only a practical convention.

Review dates show when the explanation, source links, and implementation were last checked editorially. They do not mean the page was medically reviewed. If a result or explanation appears wrong, use the Zalea Studio contact page and include the page, inputs, received result, and expected result without sending medical records.

These calculators provide general estimates for educational purposes only and are not medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for guidance about your health.
