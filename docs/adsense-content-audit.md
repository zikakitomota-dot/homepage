# Zalea Studio AdSense Content Audit

Audit date: 12 September 2026  
Audited surface: `phase2-navigation-homepage` / `https://zaleastudio.com`  
Status: Phase 1 only — no page, route, indexing, advertising, calculator, game, or design changes have been made.

## Scope and method

This audit covers the 64 canonical URLs generated by `app/sitemap.ts`, the route tree under `app`, shared content/configuration in `lib` and `components`, the current robots and redirect rules, the centralized AdSense allowlist, and every internal page link found in the rendered sitemap pages.

The site was crawled from a local production build. The word figures below are approximate counts of visible text inside `<main>` and include headings and interface labels. They are useful for comparison, not a recommendation to target a particular word count. Quality ratings consider the page's actual utility as well as prose: an interactive calculator, game, or substantive printable can provide value that a raw word count does not capture.

## Executive summary

- All 64 sitemap URLs returned HTTP 200.
- Every sitemap page had exactly one visible H1, a self-referencing canonical, and `index, follow`.
- No dead internal page links were found. All discovered internal page links returned HTTP 200.
- The sitemap contains no parameter, filter, API, redirect-alias, premium-game, or download-file URLs.
- The strict AdSense allowlist is working as a conservative boundary: only the homepage and finance, work/time, education, and math hubs/calculators are eligible. Games, child-focused guides, freebies, shop/product, contact, About, and legal pages remain ad-free.
- Work/time, education, and math calculators are already the strongest informational pages on the main domain. Most contain roughly 580–990 visible words, formulas, examples, interpretation guidance, FAQs, and related-tool links.
- Five finance calculators are useful but comparatively shallow and structurally repetitive: Discount, Split Bill, Savings Goal, Unit Price, and Cashback. Fuel Cost is substantially stronger.
- The ten free English game pages share a template, but each has a distinct grammar explanation, examples, common mistakes, practice activity, and interactive question bank. They are acceptable indexable resources and correctly remain ad-free.
- Freebie detail pages share a deliberate template and contain roughly 297–385 words each. Their main value is the original downloadable resource, supported by specific usage guidance and safety/limitation notes. They should remain indexable and ad-free.
- `/games/science/animal-habitats` is the thinnest substantive learning page at about 214 words. The game itself is useful, but its supporting educational content is noticeably lighter than Addition Level 1 and the English games.
- Contact is only about 73 words, but it is a functional trust page with a working form and clear contact reasons. It should not be padded merely to increase word count.
- About already contains a concise, honest editorial/testing methodology and identifies Zalea Studio as publisher/operator. A separate `/content-methodology` page is not currently necessary unless the existing material is intentionally expanded and moved rather than duplicated.
- Privacy Policy and Terms are substantial, visible in the footer, and consistent with the site's current analytics, advertising, purchase, game, and hosting architecture.
- No additional sitemap page currently warrants `noindex` solely for content quality.

## Public routes outside the indexable inventory

These should not be added to the sitemap merely to increase page count:

- `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/money`, and `/money/:path+` are permanent redirect aliases to canonical routes.
- `/images/english-games-social` and `/images/english-games-pinterest` are permanent compatibility redirects to existing PNG assets.
- `/api/*` routes carry `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`.
- Premium English game slugs resolve to access/locked states and explicitly use `noindex, follow`. They are intentionally absent from the sitemap.
- Download files under `/downloads` are assets, not content pages.
- There is no internal-search or indexable filter-result route.

## Duplicate and near-duplicate patterns

### Finance calculator template

The six finance calculators use the same headings: “What this calculator does,” “How to use it,” “Calculation method,” “Simple example,” “Assumptions used,” “Important limitations,” and FAQ. The inputs, formulas, examples, assumptions and answers are specific, so this is not duplicate content in a strict sense. However, five pages are short enough that the shared structure is more visible than their unique subject matter. Each should gain subject-specific interpretation and practical decision guidance rather than more template cards.

### Freebie detail template

All 13 freebie pages use the same section order. The actual included pages, learning/planning purpose, instructions, tips, audience, notices and related resources vary. This repetition is acceptable for a product/download collection, but future additions should continue to include genuinely specific copy rather than merely changing the title and filename.

### English game detail template

The ten games share layout and repeated “How to play”/Academy sections. Their central guides are distinct: each explains a different rule, gives different examples and common errors, and proposes a different offline activity. Avoid adding generic FAQ blocks to every game; improve an individual page only when that grammar skill needs more explanation.

### Overlapping English landing pages

- `/games/english` is a catalogue.
- `/english-games-for-kids` is a strong parent/teacher pathway guide.
- `/vocabulary-games-for-kids` is a strong standalone vocabulary guide.
- `/grammar-games-for-kids` currently repeats much of the catalogue and short descriptions. It needs a more distinct purpose, such as explaining how to choose and sequence grammar practice, without copying the broader English guide.

## Internal linking audit

### What is working

- No dead internal page links were found.
- Homepage links into all major content areas.
- Category hubs link to every calculator in their group.
- Work/time, education and math calculators link laterally to related calculators.
- Freebies connect to related downloads and, where relevant, calculators or games.
- English games link to the game catalogue, grammar guide, English guide and Academy.
- Breadcrumbs exist on learning/game pages and many detailed tool pages.
- About, Contact, Privacy Policy and Terms are available from the global footer.

### Gaps

- Finance calculator pages currently lack a dedicated related-resources section in the shared template.
- The site has no guides hub to connect informational search intent with calculators.
- The homepage cannot link to guides until a useful collection exists.
- Source/method attribution is mostly explained at site level on About; individual pages rarely identify a review date or source basis.
- Some calculator page families use breadcrumbs while the shared finance template uses only an “All Finance Tools” back link. This is a consistency issue, not a blocker.

## Titles, descriptions, H1s, canonicals and structured data

### Findings

- All 64 sitemap pages have one H1.
- Titles, H1s and descriptions consistently describe the same page intent.
- All canonical URLs point to the matching `https://zaleastudio.com` route.
- All audited pages use `index, follow`; no accidental `noindex` was found.
- Global Organization and WebSite JSON-LD is present.
- Work/time, education, math and game pages commonly include subject-specific structured data.
- Freebie pages include breadcrumb structured data.
- Finance calculator pages do not currently expose subject-specific calculator/FAQ structured data. This is not required for AdSense and should not be added unless it accurately reflects visible content.

### Technical concern

`app/sitemap.ts` assigns the same hard-coded `lastModified` date (`2026-08-14`) to every URL, including pages changed or added later. This does not create thin content, but it weakens the accuracy of the sitemap. Any fix should use truthful per-page dates or omit `lastModified`; it should not emit the build date for unchanged content.

## Trust and quality signals

### Present and accessible

- About page identifies Zalea Studio as publisher/operator.
- About explains research, testing, limitations, correction handling and privacy at a concise level.
- Contact provides a real form for errors, requests, feedback and business enquiries.
- Privacy Policy covers Analytics, AdSense, Cloudflare, local storage, Academy access and children's privacy.
- Terms cover educational purpose, limitations, purchases, licences, refunds and external services.
- Copyright appears in the global footer.
- Calculator pages include subject-specific limitations; money pages state general-information limits.
- Child-directed routes remain ad-free under the centralized allowlist.

### Opportunities

- Add a lightweight page attribution/review pattern to high-value informational and calculator pages, using only truthful claims (for example, “Published by Zalea Studio” and an actual reviewed date).
- Add external sources only where a standard, policy or jurisdiction materially affects the calculation. Do not decorate basic arithmetic pages with irrelevant citations.
- Keep legal and limitation language close to results where interpretation could cause harm or confusion.
- A separate `/content-methodology` route is not currently recommended: the About page already performs that role. If a standalone methodology page is approved later, the About material should be refactored rather than duplicated.
- A standalone `/disclaimer` route is also not currently recommended solely for AdSense. The relevant limitations already appear in Terms and on calculator pages.

## Indexing and AdSense suitability

### Noindex recommendations

No additional canonical sitemap page should be noindexed at this stage.

Continue the existing treatment for:

- APIs: `noindex, nofollow` via response header.
- Premium locked game routes: `noindex, follow` and excluded from sitemap.
- Redirect aliases: redirect to the canonical route.
- Static download files: remain assets rather than indexable landing pages.

### Routes that should remain excluded from AdSense

- `/games` and all `/games/*` routes.
- `/english-games-for-kids`, `/grammar-games-for-kids`, and `/vocabulary-games-for-kids`.
- `/freebies` and all `/freebies/*` routes.
- `/shop` and all `/shop/*` routes.
- `/contact`, `/about`, `/privacy-policy`, `/terms-of-use`, redirect aliases, APIs and error routes.
- Any future child-directed guide, even if its written content is strong.

The current allowlist already implements this conservative policy. New `/guides/*` routes should not automatically become AdSense-eligible; each should be reviewed and explicitly added only if appropriate.

4. `/finance/cashback-calculator` — deepen cap/effective-rate explanation and compare cashback with an immediate discount without promotional claims.
5. `/finance/unit-price-calculator` — add pack-size/conversion examples and cases where unit price is not the only decision factor.
6. `/games/science/animal-habitats` — add concise habitat-specific teaching context, misconceptions and offline observation ideas while keeping the game ad-free.
7. `/grammar-games-for-kids` — differentiate it from the catalogue with parent/teacher guidance on sequencing and recognising which grammar clue a child needs.

Fuel Cost and all current work/time, education, and math calculators should be preserved unless a factual or usability issue is found during implementation. They do not need generic expansion for word count.
